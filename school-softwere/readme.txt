Complete School Management System for Guidance Modern Academy (English Medium).

== Description ==

GMA School Management is a comprehensive WordPress plugin designed for Guidance Modern Academy (English Medium). It provides a complete school ERP system with:

**Core/Admin Manager**
* Multi-school setup and management
* Academic sessions management
* Classes & sections configuration
* Global plugin settings & wizard

**General School Admin**
* Students enrollment & profiles
* Admissions processing (with public form)
* Staff management with roles & permissions
* Certificates, ID cards, transfer certificates
* Student promotions & transfers
* Pre-admission inquiries
* Notifications & alerts
* Staff attendance & leave management

**Class Management**
* Subjects & timetable (student + staff)
* Homework assignments
* Study material uploads
* Notices & events
* Student leave requests
* Chapters & lectures

**Examinations**
* Exam scheduling & groups
* Result entry & report cards
* Admit card generation (individual & bulk)
* Assessment/grading configuration
* Academic performance reports

**Accounting**
* Fee structures & recurring fees
* Invoice generation
* Payment collection & history
* Concession types & student concessions
* Income & expense tracking with categories
* Financial reports

**Library**
* Book catalog management
* Book issuance & return tracking
* Library card generation

**Hostel**
* Hostel setup & room management

**Transport**
* Routes, vehicles & stop management

**Support**
* Internal helpdesk ticket system

**Print/Export**
* Invoices, admit cards, ID cards, certificates, TCs

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `gma-school` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress
3. The Setup Wizard will launch automatically on first activation
4. Follow the wizard to configure your school
